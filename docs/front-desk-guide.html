Rosebud

Front desk guide: printing pre-filled Routing Forms from RevolutionEHR

Setting it up (once)

Every computer runs Rosebud from one folder on the office's shared drive, so a new version is put in place once for all of them.

The shared folder (office manager, once)

  1. Download rosebud-extension-0.1.4.zip from github.com/drnickearls/rosebud/releases/latest (under Assets).
  2. Unzip it into a folder on the shared drive, for example S:\Rosebud. Don't move or rename the folder afterwards.

Every computer must reach the folder at the same drive letter and path, and the shared drive must be connected when the browser starts.

Each computer (once)

  1. In the browser, open the extensions page: type edge://extensions (Edge) or chrome://extensions (Chrome) in the address bar.
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the shared folder.
  4. Reload your RevolutionEHR tab and sign in again. The rose-colored button appears at the bottom left of the Patient Summary and the Schedule.

A computer that has its own copy of Rosebud (for example in C:\Rosebud): on the extensions page click Remove on the Rosebud card first, then follow the steps above, then delete the old folder. Removing Rosebud also removes a form layout saved on that computer; if it should be kept, use Share form on the settings page before removing.

Updating to a new version

  1. Office manager, once: download the new zip and unzip it into the shared folder, choosing Replace for the files that already exist.
  2. Each computer: click the circular reload arrow on the Rosebud card on the extensions page (or close and reopen the browser), then press F5 on RevolutionEHR and sign in again.

If the Rosebud button is missing or the extensions page shows an error on the Rosebud card, the shared drive probably wasn't connected when the browser started. Open the shared drive in File Explorer, then click reload on the Rosebud card.

Changing the form (office manager)

Click the Rosebud button in the browser's toolbar (or the extension's Details → Extension options) to open the settings page. Move the fields, change sizes, or choose a new blank form, then Test print and Save. Save changes the form on that computer only.

To use the same form on every computer:

  1. On the computer where you made the change, click Share form. It saves a form file into the forms folder inside the Rosebud folder.
  2. On each other computer, open the settings page, click Load a form file, choose that file, check it with Test print, and click Save.

The first time on each computer, the Share and Load windows open in Documents: go to the forms folder inside the Rosebud folder (for example S:\Rosebud\forms). After that, that computer opens the forms folder by itself. Choose a new blank form always opens in Downloads.

What it does

Rosebud fills in the top of the Routing Form for you — the date, appointment time, patient name, D.O.B., age, ID #, balance due and the reason for visit — and opens it as a PDF to print. Everything else on the form is left blank for handwriting.

It only reads from RevolutionEHR. It never changes a chart, an appointment or the schedule, and it does not store or send any patient information.

Print one patient's form (from their chart)

  1. Open the patient's chart (Patient Summary page).
  2. Click the Routing Form button in the bottom-left corner of the screen.
  3. The form opens in a new browser tab. Press Ctrl + P to print it.

If the patient has already been checked in today and also has a later appointment, Rosebud asks which one the form is for: Today's appointment or The next appointment. Click the one you want.

The form is made for the patient's earliest appointment on that day. A patient with no appointment gets a message instead of a form.

Print a whole day's forms (from the schedule)

  1. Open the Schedule and go to the day you want (you can print a few days ahead).
  2. Make sure the providers you want are showing on the screen.
  3. Click the Routing Forms button in the bottom-left corner.
    If it says "Click the schedule's refresh button once", click the refresh icon at the top right of the schedule, then click Routing Forms again.
  4. Tick the providers you want and click Print the selected providers.
  5. Watch the box above the button: it shows "Routing Forms completed: 5 of 12". When it finishes, the forms open in a new tab. Press Ctrl + P to print.

The printout is grouped by provider. Each provider starts with a cover page (provider name, number of exams, and the list of patients with times). After the cover page come that provider's forms in appointment-time order. Then the next provider's cover page and forms.

Just one patient from the schedule: click that patient's appointment so it opens in the panel on the right, click Routing Forms, then choose Print only (patient's name).

The Stop button in the box cancels a run that is in progress; nothing is printed.

A message that only says the forms opened fades on its own after 5 seconds (holding the mouse over it keeps it). A warning, an error or an Open the PDF link stays until you click Close.

Reason for visit (RFV)

To have the reason for the visit printed on the form, type RFV and the reason in the appointment's Comments box, and press Enter before any other notes:

RFV: 1 month post op
original surgery 8-11-26
co-management forms scanned in

The form then shows the appointment type and the reason: Cataract Post Op Exam — 1 month post op. Only the text after RFV, up to the end of that line, is used. Other notes are never printed. RFV is best on the first line but is found anywhere in the Comments. With no RFV, the form shows just the appointment type.
